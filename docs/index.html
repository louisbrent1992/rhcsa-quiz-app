Privacy Policy for RHCSA Quiz

Last updated: 10 September 2026

RHCSA Quiz ("the app") is an offline study application for the Red Hat Certified System Administrator exam objectives. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information. In short: the app collects nothing, transmits nothing, and shares nothing.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal or sensitive user data. It does not ask you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your study progress — such as which questions you have answered and your scores — locally on your device so that it can be restored the next time you open the app. This information is stored in the app's own private storage area, is accessible only to the app, and is never transmitted off the device. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes it.

Network access

The app operates entirely offline. All quiz questions are bundled inside the app itself. The app requests no Android permissions and makes no network connections.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no third-party tracking software of any kind. No data is shared with any third party, because no data is gathered in the first place.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for a general audience preparing for a professional certification. It knowingly collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted at this same address with an updated revision date above.
